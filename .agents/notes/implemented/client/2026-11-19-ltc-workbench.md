# LTC workbench previews

The workbench is a separate browser plugin registered above knowledge reviews. Its ten illustrative process nodes cover business development and operations. Solutions and Pre-visit plan open local previews without executing model tasks; remaining capabilities use disabled native buttons. Availability does not represent project completion. Stage filters affect presentation only, and all product labels belong to typed Chinese and English dictionaries.

The component test covers stage filtering, disabled future capabilities and both preview open/close paths. No durable types or model-visible inputs change.
